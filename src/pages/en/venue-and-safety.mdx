## Getting to the venue

Parking support is not available for event attendees, so please use public transportation if possible.

### By public transportation

The venue is within walking distance of **Yeoksam Station** on Line 2 or **Seolleung Station**, an interchange station for Line 2 and the Suin-Bundang Line.

## Health and Safety Information

To provide a safe environment for everyone, all attendees must follow our [Code of Conduct](/en/about). Please wear your name badge when entering the venue and attending separately registered workshops or social events such as the conference dinner and group tour. Anyone without a name badge may be asked to leave or denied entry.

### Emergency Calls

- [Police: 112](tel:112)
- [Fire/Ambulance: 119](tel:119)
- [Travel Helpline: 1330](tel:1330)
